Privacy Policy

Sales Tax Nexus. Last updated 29 August 2026.

The short version

Sales Tax Nexus collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and it makes no network request of any kind. Everything you put into it stays in one file on your device and is never sent anywhere, because the app has no way to send it.

What the app stores

One file, in the app's own container on your device: the orders you imported, the state registrations you typed in, and a record that you were shown the disclaimer. That is the whole list.

Your buyers' personal details never arrive. When you import a CSV, the app reads only the columns it declares — order number, date, destination state, amount, and whether the marketplace collected the tax. A buyer's name, email, telephone or address is unreachable from the code that reads your file, and the stored format has no field that could hold one.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you send it. The app has no networking of any kind. The only file it ever reads is the CSV you pick yourself, through the system's document picker, at the moment you pick it; access to that file is released as soon as it has been read. When you export a statement, iOS's own share sheet hands it wherever you choose — the app does not upload it.

The state thresholds the app shows are copied into the app itself before it ships. It does not fetch them, so using the app tells no state department, and no one else, that you are looking.

Your data is yours

Settings → Your data has two controls, and neither asks anyone's permission:

  • Download everything this app holds. You get the file exactly as it is stored — not a report, the data itself, in a format another program can read.
  • Delete my data. It removes every order and every registration, and removes any statement still waiting to be shared. One thing is kept and the screen says so: the record that you were shown the disclaimer, which holds a version and a date and nothing else.

Deleting the app deletes the file with it. There is no copy anywhere else for us to hold, so there is nothing to ask us to erase.

How the file is protected

The file is written with iOS's strongest file protection, which is stronger than the default: the system is asked to keep it encrypted whenever the device is locked, rather than only until the first unlock after a restart. The app does no background work at all, which is what makes that setting possible.

Payments

The subscription is sold and charged by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees a card number, a name or a billing address; it asks the App Store one question — whether this Apple Account is entitled — and the answer is read from the device.

Children

This is a business record-keeping tool and it is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.
